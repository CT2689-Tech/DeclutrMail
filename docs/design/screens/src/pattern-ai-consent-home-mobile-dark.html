Use AI to explain suggestions?

DeclutrMail can use Anthropic, an AI company, to write a short explanation for each suggestion. Here’s exactly what it would send.

Suggestion explanations

For each sender we explain, DeclutrMail sends the sender’s name and email domain, the suggested choice with the rule behind it and how sure it is, how much they email you and how much of it is marked read, and Gmail’s category for them (like Promotions). It never sends subject lines or preview snippets for this.

  • Anthropic never receives the full contents of an email, attachments or images.
  • We never fetch or store full email contents.
  • Anthropic only uses what we send to write this text for you.
  • We don’t use your email to train AI models. Anthropic sets its own retention and training terms.

If you choose Not now, you still get an explanation for each suggestion, written from our rules without AI.

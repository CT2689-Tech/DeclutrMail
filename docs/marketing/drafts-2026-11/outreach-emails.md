# Outreach pitches (drafts, checked 2026-11-18)

One draft per target. Nothing has been sent, submitted or signed up for.

**Targets** come from a Similarweb snapshot kept locally, not in the repo. These sites send competitors visits or rank for the head terms. That is Similarweb panel data. It is not a source for any sentence in a pitch.

**Contact pages and articles** were read with a web fetch on 2026-11-18, unless a row says otherwise. Re-read the contact page and its rules on the day you send. They change.

## Rules for every pitch

- Sign as `[PUBLIC NAME]`, Founder, DeclutrMail. Never a real name until the founder decides.
- Product facts only from `llms-source.ts`, `privacy.ts` and `pricing.config.ts`. Quote the trust line exactly: "We never fetch or store full email contents."
- Never offer payment, a link swap, an affiliate deal or a guest post in exchange for a link. Never ask for a dofollow link.
- Never characterise another product, and never claim a site "mentions" a competitor beyond the page title cited below.
- One email per site, and one polite follow-up after 10 days at most. No follow-up if the site's rules say not to.
- Keep any statement about a page to what the cited page says. Re-open the page before sending, because articles get updated.

### 2026-09-26 — Decide what Redis may keep of a mailbox's sync counts after "delete my data"
**Source:** privacy-auditor on the scan-count PR (feat/d109-sync-gate-progress-count), 2026-09-26, two rounds
**Why:** The D245 registry files per-sync counts under `processing-and-retry-records` ("keep counts and timings for each mailbox sync") with `removalTrigger: 'delete-indexed-data'` (packages/shared/src/contracts/gmail-data-inventory.ts). Three Redis copies are not removed that way, checked in source on 2026-09-26: (1) the mailbox's initial-sync BullMQ job (its id is the mailbox id). A completed job keeps its result, including `messagesSynced`, for up to 24h — `removeOnComplete: { age: 86_400 }` is applied when a later initial-sync job completes, and a reconnect can replace the job sooner. A failed job has no result, but keeps its `failedReason` indefinitely (`removeOnFail: false`, `initialSyncJobOptions` in packages/workers/src/queue.ts), and one failure's reason carries counts: "Gmail refused metadata for N of M messages" (packages/workers/src/initial-sync.worker.ts). (2) BullMQ writes each finish — the result, or the failure reason — into the queue's events stream (bullmq 6.3.1 `moveToFinished-14.lua`), trimmed only by length. (3) The scan-count PR's own key, `declutr:scan-progress:<mailboxId>`, holding `{processed, total, at}`: a purge that kills a scan mid-read leaves it until it expires, 30 minutes after its last write. Nothing in the app can read it by then — the status route 404s once the purge deletes the sync row — but it is still a copy. The deletion worker uses only the email-send queue (packages/workers/src/deletion.worker.ts). (1) and (2) are pre-existing.
**Update 2026-09-27:** (3) is now in the D245 registry as `scan-progress-counts` (`storageRefs: redis:declutr:scan-progress:{mailboxAccountId}`, `removalTrigger: 'retention-policy'`, shown on Settings → Privacy & data), tied by a contract test to the key the worker writes. The open decision for it is whether "delete my data" should also clear it immediately — if yes, one `DEL` in the deletion worker and `removalTrigger: 'delete-indexed-data'`. (1) and (2) are unchanged.

**Update 2026-09-28 — (3) resolved, (1) and (2) still open.** Founder decision: yes, clear it immediately. `AccountDeletionPurgeWorker.clearScanProgress` now calls `ScanProgressStore.write(mailboxAccountId, null)` (a `DEL`) for every purged mailbox, on both the mailbox-only and full-account paths — best-effort, same as the watch-stop step, so a Redis outage costs only this clear, never the purge. `removalTrigger` moved to `'delete-indexed-data'`; the registry entry moved from `GMAIL_INDEXED_DATA_DELETION_RETAINED_INVENTORY` to `GMAIL_INDEXED_DATA_DELETION_INVENTORY`, which also corrects the mailbox-exit preview dialog — "Scan progress counts" now lists under "Deleted from DeclutrMail" instead of "Retained after deletion". (1) the BullMQ initial-sync job result/failure-reason and (2) its events stream are unchanged and still need (a) or (b) from "How" below.
**How:** Retention is Tier 1, so pick one: (a) record the Redis copies and their real retention in the registry (a model change — `storageRefs` holds only DB columns today); or (b) make mailbox-data deletion remove the mailbox's initial-sync job and its scan-count key, and cap the initial-sync queue's events stream (`streams.events.maxLen`).
**Verifies by:** After deleting a test mailbox's saved data, `redis-cli EXISTS bull:initial-sync:<mailboxId>` and `redis-cli EXISTS declutr:scan-progress:<mailboxId>` both return 0, and `redis-cli XRANGE bull:initial-sync:events - +` holds no entry for that job id — or the registry states the Redis retention the app actually has. Make the check fail first: run it against a mailbox whose scan finished minutes ago, before any deletion, and see it find the job.
**Status:** Open

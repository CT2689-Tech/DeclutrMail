## Cohesion and state coverage

- Keep the five navigation groups and all child routes. Clarify vocabulary within groups: Clean up is the workspace; Senders is the explorer; Triage is the focused daily task; New senders is what Screener contains. Do not describe Screener as blocking delivery.
- Standardize a visible scope sentence where it affects decisions: active mailbox vs all mailboxes, inbox vs all mail, daily queue vs entire library, 90-day data vs lifetime history. Scope is more valuable than generic editorial filler.
- Do not add a generic KPI strip to every screen. Home orients, Senders investigates, Triage decides, Autopilot manages rules, Catch up surfaces attention, Activity verifies outcomes, Settings configures.
- Maintain feature identity through loading/error states. Follow-ups and Later early-return before their main heading (`followups-screen.tsx:80`, `snoozed-screen.tsx:108`); their loading wrappers use max-width 880 and their error wrappers 720, without the ready-state h1 (`followups-screen.tsx:412–452`, `snoozed-screen.tsx:629–668`). Retaining one header/container around the body would reduce context and width shifts.
- Existing useful story states inspected: Home new user/sync failed/loading/error; Later populated/mobile/count syncing/loading/error; Quiet configured/quiet now/crosses midnight/error/saving/disconnected; Brief populated/mobile/quiet inbox; Follow-ups populated/empty/all overdue/mobile; Autopilot activation preview/loading/error and Brief noise partial failure/unconfirmed. These are verification fixtures, not production evidence.
- Browser priorities: 320/390px subject and identity readability; 667px contextual navigation; 900px pane transition; dark-theme contrast and focus; keyboard access to summaries/disclosures; large numbers and long addresses. No overflow or contrast claim is certified by this source review.

## Suggested sequence

1. Scope/recovery quick wins: Home first-use work visibility; Screener explanation; Quiet mailbox scope; Admin reset; Follow-up subject accessibility; Later empty-state link/timezone.
2. Information hierarchy: Activity scope grouping; Senders toolbar cleanup; sender evidence grouping; Autopilot state filters; Brief jump index.
3. Supporting journeys: Settings index, searchable glossary, protection-management cross-link, phase-aware onboarding.
4. Only then contract-level work: complete new-sender search/paging if needed, precise freshness timestamps, richer scheduling. Validate demand before expanding the product.

## Parent browser observations received during review

- Triage initially paints Focus, then switches to the persisted List preference after hydration. Treat initial mode stability as a higher-priority client quick win than renaming the mode switch. Preserve persisted preference and avoid a large content swap after first paint; independently verify the preference initialization path before changing it.
- Screener includes senders awaiting a first decision whose first-seen date is months old. “New” should explicitly mean **new to review**, not recently received. Existing first-seen evidence should remain visible. This strengthens the recommendation for a persistent subtitle; no private sender examples are recorded here.

# Billing policy decisions

The user approved blocking account deletion until billing is safely stopped. The implementation never cancels or refunds a subscription as a deletion side effect. Unknown checkout outcomes require support. On October 10 the founder also approved reversing only an upgrade for a full refund of its charge, keeping allocated paid Founding seats permanently used, and provider receipts/invoices plus DeclutrMail cancellation, pause, access-deadline and grant-expiry notices. These policies are accepted in ADR-0044; implementation and release evidence are tracked separately. No automatic upgrade restoration or new email delivery is enabled by this documentation update.

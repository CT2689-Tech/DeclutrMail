Read-only main workflow [38089520530](https://github.com/CT2689-Tech/DeclutrMail/actions/runs/38089520530) then verified the exact transaction/subscription/customer linkage. Its safe projection confirms the completed upgrade has target quantity +1, prior quantity -1, corresponding positive/negative line totals, a root billing period, null item proration and absent line proration. It also freshly confirms the subscription stopped. Signed fields were present; that diagnostic does not claim to verify their HMACs. No raw custom data, signatures, email, payment data or totals were retained. The API shape agrees with the delivered webhook shape. The cause of Paddle omitting proration remains unknown; no rounding explanation is assumed.

The adapter now represents this shape explicitly as `unproratedSwap`. Both raw item arrays must agree on exactly two distinct price/quantity entries, and every proration field must be absent or null. Invalid, partially present or mismatched evidence cannot take this path. No rate is synthesized.

The policy accepts that evidence only with both verified server signatures, the exact frozen intent/subscription/target, a completed positive charge created inside the original operation window, and one target debit plus one prior credit. The root period must equal both frozen snapshots; billing cycles, original renewal, discounts and stop state must remain unchanged. Cross-cycle cases cannot use this path. Complete discovery now treats an unsigned or differently attributed second artifact as ambiguity rather than silently discarding it. Existing full-refund coverage, chargeback/cancellation precedence, fresh revision checks, no-charge preview/write/readback and canonical completion continue to apply.

Three new regressions failed before the fix: absent-proration normalization, canonical binding/restoration, and a second unsigned pending candidate. The end-to-end unit regression reads the observed raw API shape with fresh synthetic HMACs through the actual adapter, binds the charge, projects the refund restoration and proves replay owns only one no-charge write. Negative cases cover mismatched periods/cycles/renewal/discount/items/quantity/signs, malformed/partial proration, tampered signatures, later inherited nonce reuse and cancellation during preview.

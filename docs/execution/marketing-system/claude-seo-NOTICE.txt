Adapted as a small Node.js implementation, without installing Claude Code,
its agent configuration, Python runtime, browser, hooks, or paid extensions:
- skills/seo-drift/SKILL.md and scripts/drift_compare.py:
  observed metadata baselines, explicit comparisons, severity and next actions.
- skills/seo-backlinks/SKILL.md and scripts/verify_backlinks.py:
  known-link verification, anchor/rel capture, source labels and uncertainty.

DeclutrMail-specific additions: private atomic state, single-writer lock,
deduplicated tasks, evidence imports, weekly decisions, strict bounded public
reads, and publication/outreach approval requirements. Numeric backlink health
scores and generic anchor/TLD thresholds were not adopted. Baselines are
observations, not evidence of SEO quality. Missing static markup/links require
rendered confirmation. Existing Codex browser and access rules take precedence.

No upstream framework-integration material is included.

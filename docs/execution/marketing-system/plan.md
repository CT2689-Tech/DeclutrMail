# DeclutrMail marketing execution plan

Updated October 20, 2026. Owner and integration owner: the current Codex marketing
session. The founder approves external communications and spending. This plan extends
the existing daily growth loop; it does not launch another scheduler or replace its
private measurement history.

The objective is more qualified Gmail connections, successful previewed first cleanups,
verified purchases, and retained paid use. Matching Ryze's feature list is a capability
target. Being better than Ryze is an unproven outcome hypothesis: we have no matched
comparison of customer acquisition cost, conversion, retention, or operating time.

## Delivery now

1. Reuse the installed SEO, content, copy, CRO, onboarding, attribution, customer
   research, social, image/video, and directory skills. Install only the ten missing
   specialists in [skills.json](skills.json), from a pinned revision. Verify copied
   files against that revision and retain their original instructions and references.
2. Reconcile the marketing runbook with the live Free, Backlog Pass, Plus, Pro-waitlist,
   and refund policy. Preserve unverified launch and checkout gates.
3. Capture a private baseline from anonymous public HTML and authenticated Search
   Console. Read the rendered pricing page and available AI-features report. Keep
   private analytics, customer classification, mailbox information, and billing data
   outside repository files and PR descriptions.
4. Prepare three creative concepts, one demo storyboard, content briefs, and two
   distribution proposals in [drafts.md](drafts.md). These are untested drafts, not
   approved campaigns or performance winners.
5. Extend the existing daily automation to read this plan and its private evidence.
   Each run selects a supported task, verifies the result, and updates delivery state.

### Corrections that override imported advice

- The live Search Console account exposes a generative-AI-features performance report.
  The installed `ai-seo` version's assertion that no such report exists is outdated.
  Inspect the actual account and current official documentation on every relevant run.
- No fixed page-production quota, link quota, simultaneous-test quota, or promised lift.
  In particular, the `ads` suggestion to always run three split tests does not fit a
  small sample without a viable design.
- Do not interpret generic audience-research advice as permission to collect personal
  identifiers, call leads, upload customer lists, add matching pixels, or bypass consent.
- Skill links to paid partners are vendor suggestions. They create no purchase
  authorization and no preference over existing/free alternatives.
- Google does not require special AI files or markup for Search inclusion. Existing
  machine-readable product pages can serve other consumers, but are not evidence of
  improved rankings. Do not change training-crawler policy merely to chase citations.

## Measurement and benchmark

The primary chain is qualified discovery → Gmail connection → successful previewed
cleanup → distinct verified purchase → retained paid activity. Search Console reports
discovery; first-party application records establish product events; recorded billing
and settled refunds establish payments. Keep preview evidence separate from a completed
job proxy. Keep browser consent gaps and unclassified accounts explicit.

Measure the marketing system's operating time, incremental tool spend, qualified
connections, cleanup completion, net verified purchases, and retained use. Record
evidence coverage for each. A future comparison with Ryze needs comparable channels,
traffic quality, spend, windows, outcome definitions, and attribution. We have not run
that comparison, so neither this installation nor a completed audit establishes parity
or superiority.

No arbitrary test cadence: randomization starts only after the outcome baseline,
effect worth detecting, sample size, stopping rule, and expected duration are feasible.
Before that, prioritize definite defects, current factual corrections, source-backed
research, and qualitative findings.

automation prompt names their canonical location and retains the original growth
thread, schedule, and historical evidence. Newly installed skills become discoverable
on the next turn; their reviewed files may also be read explicitly in this session.
